Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026.

Who we are

Samavik L.L.C-FZ, a licensed company in the Meydan Free Zone, Dubai, UAE. Contact: edward@samavik.com.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email and message — used solely to reply to your enquiry.
  • No analytics cookies, no tracking pixels, no advertising identifiers on this site.
  • Evening or day: if you use the switch in the top bar, your choice is kept in your browser’s local storage on your own device, so that the site opens the way you left it. It is never sent to us, and it is not a cookie. Clear your browser’s site data and it is forgotten.

What this site loads

Everything on this site comes from samavik.com itself: its pages, its pictures, and the two typefaces (Cormorant Garamond and Manrope), which we host ourselves. Nothing is requested from Google Fonts or from any other third party, and the site sets no cookies. The only request your browser makes beyond fetching the pages is the one the contact form sends to us when you press Send.

How we use it

To respond to you and, where a project follows, to run that engagement. We do not sell your data or send bulk marketing.

Storage & retention

Enquiries are delivered by email (via Resend) and retained only as long as relevant to the conversation or engagement. Ask and we delete.

Your rights

Email edward@samavik.com at any time to ask what we hold, correct it, or have it deleted.
