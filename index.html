Process

From concept to production, without the theatre

  1. 1

    Scope honestly

    Understand the problem, cut it to the smallest thing worth shipping, agree a fixed scope or a retainer. You know exactly what you’re getting and when.

  2. 2

    Build in the open

    Working software every week, not status decks. AI-leveraged development means senior output at a pace small teams can’t usually buy.

  3. 3

    Ship & stand behind it

    Deployed, documented, handed over clean, or retained and iterated. Post-launch support is part of the job, not an upsell.
