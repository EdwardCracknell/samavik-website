About Samavik

Samavik is a UAE-based software company specialising in products and platforms for the adventure sports industry. We build tools that solve real operational problems — from video delivery at busy dropzones to booking platforms for wind tunnels across Europe.

Our flagship product Cutaway automates the entire skydiving video workflow, from camera assignment on the manifest to automatic delivery to the customer's inbox. We also build custom software for leading operators including FlyTribe, BFS, and Learn To BASE Jump.
